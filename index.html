Community Day — Bringing the Community into Our Lab

What happens when a school opens its doors to the entire community? At our Atal Tinkering Lab, Community Day brings people of different ages together to explore technology, discover new possibilities, and experience the power of hands-on learning. An ordinary school library took on a new purpose, becoming a space for hands-on learning, creativity, and innovation. The journey grew even more meaningful when professors from the University of Turku, Finland, visited our Atal Tinkering Lab and interacted with students as part of an Indo-Finnish collaboration, connecting local learning with international perspectives.
