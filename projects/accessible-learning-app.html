Mobile App for Accessible Learning

By Student Team B

A group of students noticed that many classmates struggled with traditional learning materials. They set out to create an app that adapts to different learning styles and abilities.

The app includes text-to-speech, adjustable font sizes, high contrast modes, and multiple learning pathways. Students tested extensively with peers to ensure usability across different accessibility needs.

By iterating based on user feedback, they discovered that the most impactful feature was allowing students to choose their own pace and choose between visual, audio, and text-based content.

The project taught them valuable lessons about inclusive design, user testing, and the importance of considering diverse perspectives from the beginning of development.
