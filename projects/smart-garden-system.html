Smart Garden Monitoring System

By Student Team A

Students identified the challenge of maintaining optimal conditions for community garden plants. With limited resources and time, many plants suffered from inconsistent watering and care.

The team designed a low-cost sensor network using Arduino and moisture sensors to monitor conditions in real-time. The system sends alerts when intervention is needed, reducing waste and improving plant health.

Through multiple iterations, they discovered that combining soil moisture data with weather forecasts created a more intelligent watering schedule. The prototype is now being tested in three community gardens.

This project demonstrates how technology can solve practical problems while teaching students about embedded systems, data collection, and environmental stewardship.
