Sometimes, an idea that changes the way we look at everyday life begins with a simple observation.

Streetlights are a familiar part of our surroundings. We see them along roads, near schools, and throughout our neighbourhoods. But what if students could look at something so ordinary and explore ways to make it smarter?

In 2018, students from the Atal Tinkering Lab at Government High School, 18th Cross, Malleshwaram, Bengaluru, took on this challenge through their Smart Street Light project.

What followed became an important milestone in our lab's innovation journey.

From an Idea to a Project

Innovation often begins by looking at everyday things with a different perspective.

The Smart Street Light project gave students an opportunity to explore a familiar part of public infrastructure through a technological project.

Developing a project involves more than coming up with an idea. Students must work towards turning their concept into something they can demonstrate and explain.

For our students, the project became an opportunity to put their learning into practice and prepare their work for a competitive platform.

It was a step towards understanding how an idea could develop into a project worth presenting beyond the classroom.

First Place at the District Level

The Smart Street Light project was presented at the INSPIRE Award MANAK competition, where it secured first place at the district level.

This recognition marked an important achievement for our school and its students.

A competition provides an opportunity for young innovators to present their work, explain their thinking, and receive feedback from people evaluating their ideas.

During the competition, the students also interacted with the judges, presenting their project and engaging with questions about their work.

These experiences can be just as valuable as the result itself. They encourage students to understand their projects deeply, communicate their ideas clearly, and gain confidence in presenting their work.

The district-level achievement was not just recognition for a project. It was encouragement for young minds to continue exploring what they could create.

Taking the Smart Street Light to Mysuru

The journey did not end with the district-level result.

The Smart Street Light project progressed to the state-level INSPIRE Award MANAK competition in Mysuru, giving the students an opportunity to showcase their work on a larger platform.

Moving from district-level recognition to a state-level showcase was a valuable experience for the team.

It offered the students an opportunity to present their project beyond their immediate surroundings and participate in a wider environment of student innovation.

For our lab, this milestone represented the importance of creating opportunities for students to develop their ideas and take them to new platforms.

A project that began in a school learning environment had become part of a state-level competition experience.

More Than a Competition

Awards are memorable, but the learning behind them is what helps students grow.

Through the Smart Street Light project, our students experienced the process of developing an idea, presenting it to judges, earning district-level recognition, and showcasing it at the state level.

Each stage offered a new opportunity to learn.

The experience also reflected the purpose of an Atal Tinkering Lab: encouraging students to explore ideas, apply their knowledge, and develop the confidence to present their work.

These early milestones help lay the foundation for future projects, new challenges, and greater opportunities.

A Small Step Towards Bigger Possibilities

Looking back at 2018, the Smart Street Light project remains an important part of our lab's journey.

Securing first place at the district level and progressing to the state-level competition in Mysuru gave our students an opportunity to experience the wider world of innovation.

But the real value of this achievement goes beyond a position or an award.

It lies in the experience of taking an idea seriously, working towards developing it, and finding the confidence to present it to others.

Every great innovation begins with someone willing to look at an ordinary problem differently. In 2018, our Smart Street Light project became one such milestone in our journey.

And as our lab continued to grow, new ideas, projects, and opportunities would follow.

One project. One important achievement. Another chapter in our journey of innovation.
