IoT projects link the physical world to digital tools. Students decide what should be measured, how often data is needed, and how people will understand the result.

They build sensor nodes, dashboards, and alerts while learning about networks, data quality, privacy, and the limits of automation.

The goal is not to connect everything. It is to create a useful feedback loop that helps a person make a better decision.
