AI and data science help students investigate patterns that are difficult to see by looking at one observation at a time. The process starts with a meaningful question and trustworthy data.

Students learn to collect, clean, visualise, and interpret data before choosing a model. They also discuss bias, consent, explainability, and when a simple rule is better than a complex system.

Used thoughtfully, these tools can help a team understand an environment, improve a prototype, or discover a new question worth exploring.
