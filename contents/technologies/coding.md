Coding gives students a language for expressing ideas, testing rules, and automating repetitive work. It is also a powerful way to make prototypes accessible to more people.

Learners work with visual programming, web technologies, Python, and microcontroller code. They practice breaking a large problem into small functions that can be tested independently.

Whether the outcome is a game, dashboard, website, or robot behavior, software helps students turn observations into responsive experiences.
