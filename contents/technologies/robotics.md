Robotics brings together physical design and computational thinking. Students learn how a robot senses its environment, makes decisions, and produces movement.

Projects begin with simple motor control and grow into autonomous vehicles, assistive mechanisms, and challenge-based builds. Teams learn to divide complex problems into testable systems.

The most important skill is iteration: tune one mechanism, test one behavior, record the result, and keep improving the machine.
