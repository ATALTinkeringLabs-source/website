A ground station is a bridge between our lab and spacecraft overhead. Students use antennas, radio hardware, tracking software, and orbital data to predict passes and receive signals.

The work connects physics, geography, coding, electronics, and careful observation. A successful pass depends on preparation, accurate timing, and a clear understanding of what the data means.

Satellite tracking makes space technology tangible: students can plan an observation, operate a real system, and study information that has travelled from orbit to their own receiver.
