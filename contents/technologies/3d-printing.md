3D printing lets students move quickly from an idea on screen to an object they can hold, test, and improve.

Learners practice 3D modelling, understand material choices, and discover why tolerances and support structures matter. A failed print becomes useful evidence for the next design.

The lab uses this technology for enclosures, moving mechanisms, accessibility tools, and low-cost prototypes that would be difficult to make by hand.
