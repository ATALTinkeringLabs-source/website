Electronics teaches students to make invisible flows understandable. With breadboards, LEDs, switches, sensors, and multimeters, they can see how a circuit behaves and learn to diagnose it.

The lab moves from simple series and parallel circuits to transistors, motor drivers, communication modules, and custom circuit boards.

Careful measurement is part of the creative process. Students learn that a reliable invention depends on documenting connections, protecting components, and checking assumptions.
