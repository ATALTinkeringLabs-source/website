Embedded systems are small computers designed to do a specific job inside a larger product. Students learn how a microcontroller reads inputs, processes information, and controls outputs.

They explore timing, power management, communication protocols, and reliable behavior. These details turn a promising circuit into a device that works outside the classroom.

Embedded electronics powers many ATL projects, from environmental monitors to assistive tools, and teaches students to think about the whole system from sensor to user.
