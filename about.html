ABOUT

We grow curious minds into confident creators.

ATAL Tinkering Lab is a space where students explore ideas, build prototypes, and turn local challenges into meaningful solutions through hands-on learning.

Mission

To make innovation approachable.

We help learners experiment with STEM, design thinking, and real-world problem solving in an environment that encourages creativity, teamwork, and persistence.

Approach

Learn by making.

Students move from observation to concept to prototype, testing ideas in rapid cycles and refining their work with feedback from mentors and peers.

Impact

Build confidence, not just projects.

Every experiment builds problem-solving skills, collaboration, and the courage to ask better questions about the world around us.

What happens here

Hands-on learning that connects ideas to impact.

  • Design challenges and maker workshops
  • STEM exploration and collaborative prototyping
  • Mentor guidance and reflection-based learning
  • Community-focused innovation projects
